What is checked — Persian prose must render right-to-left and hug the right edge; English-only text must stay left-to-right (even inside a Persian container such as DeepSeek's .ds-markdown); list bullets must sit next to their text; a block must not flip direction from one line to the next; numbers-only and code-only blocks must be left alone.

Failure meanings — base=ltr: the block is laid out left-to-right. align=left: the Persian text hugs the left edge. marker stays left: bullets were left behind. per-line flip-flop: lines disagree about direction.
